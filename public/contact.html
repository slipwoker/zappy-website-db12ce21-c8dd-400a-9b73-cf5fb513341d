Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out. We keep it straight and simple — just like our residential plumbing services.

How fast do you respond to plumbing emergencies?

We offer 24/7 emergency response across our service area. When a pipe bursts or a drain backs up, we dispatch a licensed technician as quickly as possible — often within the hour. Don't wait; call us the moment something goes wrong.

What areas does Lone Star Plumbing Co. serve?

We proudly serve homeowners and businesses throughout the greater Texas region. Whether you're in the city or out in the suburbs, our team covers a wide service footprint. Contact us to confirm availability in your specific zip code.

Do you work on commercial properties too?

Absolutely. We handle both residential and commercial plumbing projects — from single-family homes to multi-unit buildings and small businesses. Our licensed plumbers are equipped for jobs of all sizes and complexity levels.

What information should I include in my message?

To help us respond quickly and accurately, please include your address or city, a brief description of the issue, and whether it's an emergency. Photos or a short description of what you're seeing go a long way in getting you the right expert fast.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Yes — every technician on our team is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Texas. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of action plumbing, heating, and air service so you can trust the work done in your home or business.

Do you offer free estimates?

For most non-emergency jobs, we're happy to provide a clear, upfront estimate before any work begins. We believe in transparent pricing with no hidden surprises — because trust is the foundation of every job we take on.
